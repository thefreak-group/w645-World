Popular Openings

Three of the most common chess openings — for those just getting started.

1. Italian Game

1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 Nc6 3. Bc4

One of the oldest and most straightforward openings. White quickly develops the bishop to c4, aiming it at the f7 square — a weak spot in Black's position. Black replies symmetrically.

Difficulty: easy · For White: active · For Black: solid

2. Sicilian Defence

1. e4 c5

The most popular reply for Black against 1. e4. Instead of symmetry, Black immediately fights for the centre from the flank, leading to sharp and asymmetrical positions. Seen at every level — from beginners to world champions.

Difficulty: medium · For White: space · For Black: counterplay

3. Queen's Gambit

1. d4 d5 2. c4

A classic opening for White on the queenside. White offers the c4 pawn to lure the black d5 pawn away and seize the centre. A reliable choice for positional play.

Difficulty: medium · For White: central pressure · For Black: solid position
